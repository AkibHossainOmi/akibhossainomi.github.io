About

Engineer at Telcobright, co-founder at Intech Solution.

I'm Akib, from Narayanganj, Bangladesh. I finished my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil Institute of IT, and since July 2023 I've been a software engineer at Telcobright, working on the billing and reporting side of a telecom platform.

I also co-founded Intech Solution, where we build QuickSell for shops that run their business inside chat apps.

Competitive programming is how I learned, and I still like a hard problem. I was president of the DIIT Programming Club for a year, writing problem sets and coaching teams for regional contests.

Away from the keyboard I play games, read, and travel whenever there's a chance.
